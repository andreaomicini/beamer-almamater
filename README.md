# beamer-AMSBolognaFC

> A simple Beamer-LaTeX style for presentations by Alma Mater Studiorum (Cesena
> Campus) with Bologna FC standard colours

## author

* Andrea Omicini

## this branch

AMSBolognaFC as it was installed in `~/Library/texmf` and used by the
*Distributed Systems* decks of 2022 to 2025: the theme of the time, kept so that
those decks rebuild as they were, pinned through their `.style` submodule. It is
not `main` and never merges into it.

## Four tags, the states of 2021, 1.3 and 1.5

* `bolognafc-20210830` — the style of 30 August 2021 (`d258a40`, *New speaker
  commands*), before any version number; its theme files stayed the same until
  January 2022. Pinned by every stage of `sd2122-slides` (SD 2021/2022), whose
  2021 `.bbl` and Virtuale PDFs prove it.

* `bolognafc-1.3.20220217` — the style of 17 February 2022 (`ad2d547`,
  *change version*). Pinned by every stage of `sd2223-slides` (SD 2022/2023),
  whose 2022 `.bbl` and Virtuale PDFs prove it.

* `bolognafc-1.5.20230211` — the style of 11 February 2023 (`5a6d5f9`, *apice
  option added*), its `apalike-AMS.bst` ending every entry with a period. Pinned
  by the first seven stages of `ds2324-slides` (DS 2023/2024, built up to 1
  October 2023), whose `.bbl` prove it.
* `bolognafc-1.5.20230211-nodot` — the same, with the bibliography style as
  installed later, without the trailing period (`399f8d1`). Pinned by the other
  stages of `ds2324-slides` (from 8 October 2023) and by `ds2425-slides`'
  `v1-taught`.

The tags say which state is which; the branch's head carries the later one.
